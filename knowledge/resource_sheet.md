# Catalyst Bot: Official Resource Sheet

Verified information the bot answers from. Topics are the ones workers ranked highest in the October 2026 survey: salary & work rules, and healthcare.

Last checked: 18 November 2026. Re-check every link before launch and every few months. Rules change (for example, the Primary Care Plan changes on 1 April 2027).

---

## Words workers use

Workers often use these words. They mean the same as the words in this sheet:
- worker card, WP card, permit card, work pass, work pass card, permit, IC, card → Work Permit card
- boss, company, towkay, supervisor, manager, agent's company → employer
- pay, money, wages, sambalam (Tamil), beton (Bengali) → salary
- off day, holiday (when they mean their weekly day off) → rest day
- MC, sick, medical leave → sick leave
- OT → overtime
- dorm, hostel, quarters → dormitory
- accident, injury, got hurt → work injury
- clinic, doctor, medical → healthcare / Primary Care Plan

## Salary

Summary: Your employer must pay the salary declared to MOM, no later than 7 days after the end of each month. If they don't, you can file a claim with TADM.

### When must I be paid?
Answer: No later than 7 days after the last day of the month. The salary period cannot be longer than 1 month.
Source: MOM: Paying the salary — https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/paying-the-salary

### How much must I be paid?
Answer: The fixed monthly salary your employer declared in your Work Permit application.
Source: MOM: Paying the salary — https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/paying-the-salary

### I live in a dorm. Can I be paid in cash?
Answer: No. If you live in a dormitory, salary must go directly into your bank account. If your employer keeps your bank book, you must be allowed to check it.
Source: MOM: Paying the salary — https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/paying-the-salary

### Can my employer cut my salary or add new deductions?
Answer: Only with your written consent, and they must inform MOM through WP Online. They cannot change your salary before your Work Permit is issued.
Source: MOM: Salary deductions — https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/salary/salary-deductions

### What can never be deducted?
Answer: Costs of employing you: levy, work pass renewal, security bond, medical insurance, medical fees, repatriation, compulsory training. Also, no deductions as a condition of getting or keeping the job.
Source: MOM: Salary deductions — https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/salary/salary-deductions

### What deductions are allowed?
Answer: Absence from work, accepted accommodation, advances or loans, damage or loss you caused (after an inquiry, max 25% of one month's salary), and a few others with your written consent.
Source: MOM: Salary deductions — https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/salary/salary-deductions

### Is there a limit on deductions?
Answer: Total deductions cannot be more than 50% of your salary in one month (some types, like absence and loan repayment, are not counted in this limit).
Source: MOM: Salary deductions — https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/salary/salary-deductions

### I quit. When do I get my last salary?
Answer: Within 7 days of your last day of work.
Source: MOM FAQ — https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/termination/if-i-resign-without-serving-required-notice-when-must-my-employer-pay-my-final-salary

### My salary is late or wrong. What can I do?
Answer: File a salary claim with TADM (see "Where to get help").
Source: MOM FAQ — https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/termination/what-should-i-do-if-ive-already-resigned-but-still-havent-received-my-salary

## Rest days, working hours and leave

Summary: Most workers in manual jobs earning a basic salary of $4,500 or less are covered: 1 rest day a week, overtime at 1.5x, and paid sick leave after 3 months.

### Do I get a rest day?
Answer: Yes, 1 rest day per week (midnight to midnight). It is unpaid. If it isn't Sunday, your employer should give you a monthly roster before the month starts. No more than 12 days between rest days.
Source: MOM: Hours, overtime, rest day — https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/hours-of-work-overtime-and-rest-days

### Can my employer make me work on my rest day?
Answer: Not unless there are exceptional circumstances.
Source: MOM: Hours, overtime, rest day — https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/hours-of-work-overtime-and-rest-days

### How much for working on my rest day?
Answer: If your employer asked: 1 day's pay for up to half a day, 2 days' pay for more than half a day, plus overtime for hours beyond your normal day. If you asked: half of those amounts.
Source: MOM: Hours, overtime, rest day — https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/hours-of-work-overtime-and-rest-days

### How many hours can I work?
Answer: Normal hours are up to 44 a week (8 or 9 a day). Never more than 12 hours a day except in emergencies. Max 72 overtime hours a month.
Source: MOM: Hours, overtime, rest day — https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/hours-of-work-overtime-and-rest-days

### Do I get breaks?
Answer: Usually you should not work more than 6 hours in a row without a break.
Source: MOM: Hours, overtime, rest day — https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/hours-of-work-overtime-and-rest-days

### How is overtime paid?
Answer: At least 1.5x your hourly basic rate, paid within 14 days after the end of the salary period. Workmen (manual workers) qualify if basic salary is $4,500 or less.
Source: MOM: Hours, overtime, rest day — https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/hours-of-work-overtime-and-rest-days

### Do I get sick leave?
Answer: After 3 months with your employer: up to 14 days paid outpatient sick leave and up to 60 days paid hospitalisation leave a year (the 60 includes the 14). Full amount after 6 months. You need a doctor's MC.
Source: MOM: Sick leave — https://mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/leave/sick-leave/eligibility-and-entitlement

### Do I get annual leave?
Answer: 7 days in your first year, 8 in your second, increasing with years of service.
Source: MOM: Annual leave eligibility and entitlement — https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/leave/annual-leave/eligibility-and-entitlement
Also: TWC2 fact sheet — https://twc2.org.sg/2012/01/15/fact-sheet-correct-way-to-calculate-salaries-non-domestic-workers/

### What about public holidays?
Answer: Covered employees get paid public holidays; working on one earns extra pay.
Source: MOM: Public holidays — https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/public-holidays-entitlement-and-pay

## Work Permit and work rules

Summary: A Work Permit lets you work only for your employer, in the job written on the permit. Your employer must pay for your housing, medical insurance and medical treatment.

### What am I allowed to do?
Answer: Work only for your employer, in the occupation and sector on your permit. No side business or other jobs. Live at the address your employer set, and tell them before moving.
Source: MOM: Work Permit conditions — https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/work-permit-conditions

### How long is a Work Permit?
Answer: Usually up to 2 years, depending on passport validity, security bond and contract length.
Source: MOM: Work Permit conditions — https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/work-permit-conditions

### What must my employer pay for?
Answer: Your salary, upkeep and medical treatment, proper housing, medical insurance for hospital stays and surgery, the Primary Care Plan, the foreign worker levy and the security bond.
Source: MOM: Work Permit conditions — https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/work-permit-conditions

### Can my employer or agent ask me for money to get or keep the job?
Answer: Employers must not demand or receive money in connection with your employment or change of job, and cannot deduct salary as a condition of hiring you.
Source: MOM: Work Permit conditions — https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/work-permit-conditions
Also: MOM: Salary deductions — https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/salary/salary-deductions

### How do I show my work pass?
Answer: Use the digital work pass in the SGWorkPass app, or your Work Permit card, when a public officer asks.
Source: MOM: SGWorkPass — https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/sgworkpass

### Can my employer keep my Work Permit card? (also asked as: worker card, WP card, permit card, work pass card)
Answer: No. The employer must not keep the Work Permit card. The card is issued to the worker and must be held by the worker at all times for proper identification.
Source: MOM FAQ — https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/can-an-employer-hold-on-to-a-foreign-workers-work-permit-card

### Can I change employers?
Answer: Yes, with your current employer's consent. Without consent, a new employer can apply for you only 21 to 40 days before your Work Permit expires.
Source: MOM fact-check (2024) — https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/fact-checks/2024/response-to-twc2-articles-on-ipa-for-mws
Also: MOM: Notify changes — https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/notify-mom-of-changes

### What happens when my job ends?
Answer: Your employer must cancel the permit within 1 week of your last day, pay for your flight home (with check-in luggage), and settle all salary owed.
Source: MOM: Work Permit conditions — https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/work-permit-conditions

### Can I marry a Singaporean or PR?
Answer: Only with MOM's approval, even after your permit ends.
Source: MOM: Work Permit conditions — https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/work-permit-conditions

## Workplace safety and work injury

Summary: If you are hurt at work, see a doctor and tell your supervisor as soon as possible. You can claim compensation even if your employer disagrees or your work pass is cancelled.

### I got hurt at work. What do I do first?
Answer: Get medical treatment and tell your employer or supervisor as soon as possible. If you don't, they may dispute that the injury was work-related.
Source: MOM: How to claim — https://www.mom.gov.sg/workplace-safety-and-health/work-injury-compensation/employees-how-to-claim

### My employer won't report my accident.
Answer: You can submit an incident report to MOM yourself. MOM will investigate your claim and why your employer didn't report it.
Source: MOM FAQ — https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/reporting-work-related-accidents/what-if-my-employer-feels-that-my-accident-is-not-work-related-and-does-not-submit-any-incident-report

### Can my employer send me home while my claim is open?
Answer: No, not against your wishes. If they try, or stop giving you housing or food, ask MOM for help.
Source: MOM: Employers during a claim — https://www.mom.gov.sg/workplace-safety-and-health/work-injury-compensation/employers-what-to-do-during-a-claim
Also: MOM: How to claim — https://www.mom.gov.sg/workplace-safety-and-health/work-injury-compensation/employees-how-to-claim

### How long do I have to claim?
Answer: Within 1 year of the accident, even if you no longer work there or your pass is cancelled.
Source: MOM: Employers during a claim — https://www.mom.gov.sg/workplace-safety-and-health/work-injury-compensation/employers-what-to-do-during-a-claim

### Do I need a lawyer?
Answer: No. MOM says you do not need a lawyer to claim work injury compensation.
Source: MOM: WIC resources — https://www.mom.gov.sg/workplace-safety-and-health/work-injury-compensation/resources-and-tools

### My workplace is unsafe. Where do I report it?
Answer: Tell your supervisor, or report to MOM through SnapSAFE, the FWMOMCare app, or the MOM safety hotline 6438 5122. Your employer cannot fire or threaten you for reporting.
Source: MOM: Raising WSH issues — https://mom.gov.sg/workplace-safety-and-health/safe-measures/worker-level/empowering-workers-and-members-of-public-to-raise-wsh-issues
Also: MOM: SnapSAFE — https://www.mom.gov.sg/workplace-safety-and-health/snapsafe---reporting-saves-lives

## Healthcare

Summary: Most dorm and construction, manufacturing and process workers are covered by the Primary Care Plan (PCP), which their employer pays for. A clinic visit costs the worker $5 and a telemedicine call $2 (before GST).

### What does the PCP cover?
Answer: Unlimited consultations for illnesses like flu, fever, high blood pressure and diabetes; one health screening a year; telemedicine with medicine delivery; transport between dorm and medical centre when you report sick; ambulance to hospital when needed; work pass medical exams.
Source: MOM: Getting primary care — https://www.mom.gov.sg/primary-care-plan/getting-primary-care-services

### How much do I pay?
Answer: $5 per clinic visit, $2 per telemedicine call (before GST). From 1 April 2027, both will be $5.
Source: MOM: Getting primary care — https://www.mom.gov.sg/primary-care-plan/getting-primary-care-services
Also: MOM press release (Aug 2026) — https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/press-releases/2026/0806-enhanced-primary-care-plan-appointment-of-new-aos

### How do I see a doctor?
Answer: Telemedicine is open 24 hours for non-urgent problems. For a clinic visit, call your medical centre's appointment hotline (walk-ins are limited). Clinics are closed on public holidays.
Source: MOM: Getting primary care — https://www.mom.gov.sg/primary-care-plan/getting-primary-care-services

### Which clinic do I go to?
Answer: It depends on your zone (the first 2 digits of your home postal code). MOM's page lists every clinic, address and opening hours by zone.
Source: MOM: Find a clinic — https://www.mom.gov.sg/primary-care-plan/getting-primary-care-services

### Who pays for hospital or surgery?
Answer: Your employer must buy medical insurance that covers hospital stays and surgery, and pay for your medical treatment. Medical fees cannot be deducted from your salary.
Source: MOM: Work Permit conditions — https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/work-permit-conditions
Also: MOM: Salary deductions — https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/salary/salary-deductions

### What changes in April 2027?
Answer: Six zones become four (North: SATA CommHealth, South: St Andrew's Mission Hospital, East: Healthway, West: Fullerton Health). Most workers will have a clinic within 2km, and can book or change appointments in advance.
Source: MOM press release (Aug 2026) — https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/press-releases/2026/0806-enhanced-primary-care-plan-appointment-of-new-aos

### Emergency
Answer: For an emergency, always call 995 for an ambulance.

## Where to get help

The bot ends every answer it isn't sure about with one of these contacts. MWC's helpline is the only one open 24 hours in workers' languages.

### Migrant Workers' Centre (MWC) helpline
Use it for: Any work problem: unpaid salary, housing, medical treatment, unfair treatment.
How to reach: 6536 2692
Hours: 24 hours, in about 10 languages.
Source: MWC: 24-hour helpline — https://www.mwc.org.sg/how-we-help/24-hour-helpline

### Migrant Workers' Law Centre @ MWC
Use it for: Free legal advice (with Pro Bono SG).
How to reach: Via MWC, 6536 2692
Source: MWC — https://www.mwc.org.sg/how-we-help/migrant-workers-law-centre-at-mwc

### TADM (Tripartite Alliance for Dispute Management)
Use it for: Filing a claim for unpaid or wrong salary.
How to reach: "Ask TADM" chatbot and online claim form.
Notes: Claims up to $20,000; file early. Time limits apply.
Source: TADM: About — https://www.tal.sg/tadm/about
Also: TADM: File a claim — https://www.tal.sg/tadm/eservices/employees-file-employment-claim

### Ministry of Manpower (MOM)
Use it for: Reporting salary, housing or work pass problems; unsafe work.
How to reach: 6438 5122
Hours: Mon–Fri 8.30am–5.30pm, Sat 8.30am–1pm, closed Sun and public holidays.
Source: MOM: Phone numbers — https://mom.gov.sg/contact-us/phone

### FWMOMCare app
Use it for: Ask to speak with an MOM officer, report unsafe work, find a doctor.
How to reach: Download from the app store.
Languages: English, Mandarin, Bengali, Tamil, Thai, Vietnamese.
Source: MOM: FWMOMCare — https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/fwmomcare

### SnapSAFE
Use it for: Reporting unsafe work with photos.
How to reach: Online form.
Notes: Your employer cannot fire you for reporting.
Source: MOM: SnapSAFE — https://www.mom.gov.sg/workplace-safety-and-health/snapsafe---reporting-saves-lives

### PCP telemedicine
Use it for: Non-urgent doctor consultation.
How to reach: Links by Anchor Operator on MOM's page.
Hours: 24 hours.
Source: MOM: Getting primary care — https://www.mom.gov.sg/primary-care-plan/getting-primary-care-services

### Transient Workers Count Too (TWC2)
Use it for: NGO help with salary claims, injury, food support.
How to reach: Website https://twc2.org.sg/ ; email info@twc2.org.sg ; phone/WhatsApp +65 6247 7001
Notes: Check the website for the current helpline.
Source: TWC2 — https://twc2.org.sg/

### Emergency
Use it for: Injury, fire, crime.
How to reach: 995 ambulance / fire, 999 police.
Hours: 24 hours.

## Notes for the bot

- Many workers ask their employer for salary information. When the problem is with the employer, gently point to MWC or TADM.
- Most MOM pages are in English only. These official channels support workers' languages:
  - FWMOMCare app: English, Mandarin, Bengali, Tamil, Thai, Vietnamese.
  - MWC helpline: about 10 languages, 24 hours.
  - Work injury claim form: available in English, Chinese, Tamil and Bengali, with MOM interpreters.
